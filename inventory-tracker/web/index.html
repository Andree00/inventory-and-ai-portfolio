Record a sale

Stock decreases and the current average cost is recorded as COGS.

Understanding COGS

Cost of goods sold is calculated from quantity sold × moving average item cost at the time of sale.

Example

Sell 3 units with an average cost of 20 each: COGS is 60. Revenue is the quantity multiplied by the selling price.

Sales history

Revenue and COGS are saved for each sale.
