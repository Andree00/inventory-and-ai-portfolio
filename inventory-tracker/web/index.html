Record a sale

Stock decreases and the current average cost is recorded as COGS.

Understanding COGS

Cost of goods sold is calculated from quantity sold × moving average item cost at the time of sale.

Example

Sell 3 units with an average cost of Rp20.000 each: COGS is Rp60.000. Revenue is the quantity multiplied by the selling price.

Sales history

Revenue and COGS are saved for each sale.

Recalculate open-month COGS

Replays this month’s movements from the previous closing balance. Closed months and their COGS remain unchanged.

Close the ongoing month

After the calendar month ends, close it to save the final stock and cost per item and open the next month. Closing cannot be undone in this demo.

Ongoing month: current stock and unit COGS

Live balances for the open period; they become fixed when the month is closed.

Closed-month stock and COGS

Final closing balances cannot be changed in this app. Current stock and cost appear in the item master.
